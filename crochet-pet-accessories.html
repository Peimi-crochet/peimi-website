Design direction
Motif, yarn colors, fastening method and intended wearing occasion.
Retail presentation
Brand tag, care information, display card, bag or gift-box needs.
Commercial plan
Quantity, size split, target market and required delivery window.

Pet hats, bandanas, collars, scarves and seasonal sets can be reviewed for custom colors, motifs, size ranges and packaging.

How is sizing confirmed?
