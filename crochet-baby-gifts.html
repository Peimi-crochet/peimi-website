BUYER BRIEF

What to confirm before sampling

A precise baby-product brief helps us select the correct construction, presentation and testing route before a quotation is finalized.

Market & age grade
Destination country, intended age and product use.
Product construction
Dimensions, yarn, filling, attachments and color palette.
Retail presentation
Gift box, insert, warnings, barcode and private label.
Order plan
Quantity split, target cost, launch date and delivery window.
What is the starting MOQ?

The standard starting MOQ is 200 pieces per style. Assortment and color splits can be reviewed during quotation.

Can we develop a coordinated gift set?

Yes. Characters, colors, gift boxes, inserts and labels can be planned as one retail program.

How are compliance needs handled?

Provide the destination market, age grade, intended use and required test standard before sampling. Requirements vary by product and market.
