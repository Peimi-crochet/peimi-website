OUR STORY: Craftsmanship with a Soul

Founded in 2015, PEIMI began with a simple mission: to preserve the rhythmic grace of traditional hand-knitting. Today, we empower over 2,000 rural artisans, including women and individuals with disabilities, providing a sanctuary where every needle stitch carries the warmth of a human soul. Our factory-direct model ensures ethical production and premium quality for wholesale partners worldwide.
