Buyer FAQ

Wholesale crochet car hanger questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

The standard MOQ is 200 pieces per style, with color splits reviewed during sampling.

Can the hanging details be customized?

Yes. Loop length, beads, tassels, knots, motif dimensions and packaging can be specified.

What safety information should buyers consider?

Confirm total length, weight and placement guidance. The ornament must not obstruct the driver's view and should comply with the destination market's rules.

How long does production take?

Standard bulk production is typically 15–30 days after sample approval and order confirmation.
