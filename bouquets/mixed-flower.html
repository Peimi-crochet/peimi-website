The Story Behind the Stitch

Every Mixed Flower Bouquet from PEIMI represents over 40 hours of focused dedication. As our artisans weave the soft cotton yarns, they aren't just making a product; they are preserving a rhythmic grace passed down through generations.

This particular arrangement combines the boldness of hand-knitted roses with the subtle elegance of lavender and lilies—a composition that mirrors the diversity of the southern Chinese villages where our legacy began. Each stitch carries the thumbprint of a master crafter, ensuring that no machine-made alternative can ever match its soul.
