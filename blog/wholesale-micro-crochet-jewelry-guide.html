How to Source Wholesale Micro Crochet Jewelry

Micro crochet jewelry requires precise specifications because small differences in thread, hook size and findings can change the finished look.

Define Dimensions and Weight

State the finished length and width of each piece, including the metal finding. Lightweight comfort is a key selling point, so request sample weights for larger floral designs.

Select Yarn and Findings Together

Yarn color and metal finish should be approved as one combination. Specify hook, stud, clip-on or brooch hardware and discuss destination-market material requirements.

Review Fine Details in the Sample

Inspect petal symmetry, thread ends, attachment strength, pair matching and color consistency. Earrings should be reviewed as pairs rather than individual pieces.

Design Retail-Ready Display Cards

A well-proportioned card protects the product and explains the handmade value. Reserve space for brand identity, barcode, materials and care guidance.

Build Coordinated Capsules

Matching earrings, brooches and hair accessories allow boutiques to merchandise a stronger floral story while reusing approved colors and motifs.
