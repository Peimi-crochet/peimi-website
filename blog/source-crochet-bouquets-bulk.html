How to Source Crochet Flower Bouquets in Bulk

Wholesale buyers get better samples and more accurate prices when the bouquet brief defines composition, dimensions and packaging before production.

1. Define the Retail Use

A florist may need premium wrapped arrangements, while an event buyer may prioritize uniform colors and reliable delivery. Gift shops often benefit from compact bouquets that display easily and ship efficiently. Define the sales channel, target customer and retail price first.

2. Specify the Flower Mix

List the flower types, stem count, approximate bouquet diameter and preferred colors. If exact flowers are flexible, provide a visual mood and target cost so the factory can recommend a balanced combination.

3. Approve Materials and Construction

Confirm yarn composition, internal wire, stem wrapping and any small parts. The sample should establish stitch density, flower shape, stem strength and finished weight.

4. Plan Wrapping and Branding

Wrapping paper, ribbon, message cards, barcode labels and protective cartons affect both presentation and shipping cost. Ask for a packed sample instead of approving the bouquet alone.

5. Build a Practical QC Checklist

  • Flower count and color match
  • Finished dimensions and stem alignment
  • No loose yarn ends or exposed wire
  • Consistent wrapping and label position
  • Drop and carton checks for export packaging

For an accurate quotation, send reference images, quantity, destination market, required delivery date and packaging details together.
