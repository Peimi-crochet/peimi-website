Seasonal Sourcing Guide

Wholesale Crochet Christmas Ordering Timeline

Seasonal handmade products need more planning than ready-stock goods. Work backward from your warehouse delivery date and include approval time at every stage. Confirm the actual schedule for the selected designs, quantity, packaging and destination market.

Start with the Warehouse Date

Begin with the date when sellable goods must reach your warehouse, not the date when the promotion starts. Allow time for receiving, internal checks, allocation to stores or fulfillment centers and photography. A buyer supplying several regions should decide whether the order will ship together or be divided by destination. This backward plan creates a realistic decision deadline for artwork, samples and purchase-order approval.

Concept and Range Planning

Choose the number of designs, intended retail channel, target price points, color story and packaging format. Coordinated sets should be planned together so samples can be reviewed as a collection. Separate essential features from optional ideas: character shape, hanging method and brand colors may be essential, while secondary decorations can remain flexible.

Prepare a Complete Sample Brief

A useful brief includes target dimensions, color references, front and back views, hanging loop requirements, labels, cards and retail presentation. State whether ornaments will be sold individually, in matched sets or in a gift box. The supplier can then confirm which construction details require a sample decision and which depend on quantity or destination-market requirements.

Sampling and Revision

Review the first sample against the written brief rather than relying on memory. Check silhouette, dimensions, facial details, color balance, attachment points and how the ornament hangs. Consolidate comments into one marked document so the revised sample has a clear approval target. Custom characters and printed gift packaging generally create more review points than a simple color adjustment.

Freeze the Specification Before Bulk Production

Bulk production should begin only after the buyer approves the sample and records the final specification. Confirm quantity by design, approved colors, component placement, packaging files and carton marks. Late changes can affect consistency and timing, so unresolved details should be closed before release. Availability and timing must be confirmed for the actual order instead of assumed from a previous season.

Inspection and Packaging

Build inspection points around dimensions, color, secure loops, loose threads, attached accessories, labels and presentation. For gift sets, verify that every component is included and positioned correctly. Review a packed example to confirm that the product remains tidy and protected during handling.

Freight and Delivery Buffer

Compare available shipping methods using the confirmed packed order, destination and deadline. Leave a practical buffer for export handling, customs, warehouse receiving and onward distribution. The best option depends on order size and destination; this guide does not replace a shipment-specific quotation.

Information to Send Your Supplier

  • Required warehouse delivery date and destination
  • Quantity by design and how sets should be assembled
  • Approved references, dimensions and color direction
  • Packaging, label, barcode and carton-mark files
  • Inspection priorities and receiving requirements
