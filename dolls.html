Custom Amigurumi & Crochet Dolls

PEIMI manufactures custom amigurumi dolls for toy brands, gift retailers and private-label programs. Buyers typically start with a character brief, target size, labeling plan and quantity goal so sampling, pricing and bulk production can be aligned from the first quote.

What should buyers confirm before requesting an amigurumi quote?

For wholesale crochet dolls, the most useful first answer is simple: confirm the character style, target size, packaging needs and destination market before comparing suppliers. That keeps sample feedback, lead time and labeling decisions tied to the same retail program instead of turning the quote stage into guesswork.

1. Character Direction

Send front, side or reference images, note which facial details must stay fixed, and flag whether the design is original or licensed.

2. Retail Requirements

Define size range, packaging format, label placement and destination market so the sample plan reflects the final shelf presentation.

3. Volume & Timing

The current collection uses MOQ 200 pieces per design and a standard 15 to 30 day lead time, so buyers should confirm target quantity and launch window early.

Custom Amigurumi FAQ for Wholesale Buyers

These are the buying questions we most often see before sample approval. The answers stay aligned with the production details shown on this page.

What is the MOQ for custom amigurumi orders?

The standard MOQ on this collection page is 200 pieces per design. Buyers planning mixed collections should group designs by launch priority before requesting pricing.

How long does custom amigurumi production take?

The product cards on this page list a normal lead time of 15 to 30 days. Final timing depends on sample approval speed, packaging confirmation and the number of design variants.

Can PEIMI develop private-label or branded crochet characters?

Yes. PEIMI supports OEM and ODM development for branded dolls, mascots and private-label crochet collections when buyers provide clear references and branding requirements.

What should buyers send before requesting a quote?

The fastest quote starts with a target size, character references, packaging direction, destination market and quantity target. That gives the sample plan and lead-time discussion a shared baseline.
