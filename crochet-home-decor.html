What to confirm before sampling

A complete brief helps us quote accurately and build a sample that matches the intended retail program.

Retail use
Store type, target customer and display method.
Product details
Dimensions, color palette, quantity split and material preference.
Brand presentation
Logo label, hangtag, insert, barcode and carton marks.
Delivery plan
Target market, launch date and required delivery window.
What is the starting MOQ?

The standard starting MOQ is 200 pieces per style. Color splits can be reviewed during quotation.

Can one range share the same colors?

Yes. We can coordinate yarn colors and packaging across baskets, table items and decorative accents.

How are samples approved?

We confirm dimensions, construction, colors and branding through a physical sample before bulk production.
